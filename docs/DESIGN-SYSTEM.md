# Kalna Liquor Delivery - Premium Design System

## 1. Brand Identity
The aesthetic is dark, premium, trustworthy, and locally focused.
- **Primary Vibe**: Elegant night-time convenience, exclusive yet accessible.
- **Theme**: Pure Black (`#000000`) and Deep Charcoal backgrounds.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Accent**: Amber / Gold (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`). Represents premium liquor and warmth.
- **Secondary Accent**: Sapphire Blue (`#3B82F6`) for trust and verification badges.
- **Background (App)**: Pure Black (`#000000`).
- **Surface/Card**: Dark Gray (`#111827`).
- **Text (Primary)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Text (Secondary)**: Cool Gray (`#9CA3AF`).
- **Success (Green)**: Emerald (`#10B981`) for successful deliveries and verified IDs.
- **Danger (Red)**: Rose (`#F43F5E`) for cancellations, rejections, and legal warnings.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings**: Sans-serif, bold, uppercase tracking (letter-spacing: widest) for premium feel.
- **Body**: Clean, legible sans-serif for reading product descriptions and policies.
- **Numbers/Prices**: Monospace or bold tabular figures.

## 4. Components

### Cards & Surfaces
- Flat surfaces with subtle borders (e.g., `border-gray-800`).
- Minimal shadow, relying more on borders for depth.
- High border-radius (`rounded-2xl`).

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Amber background, black text, bold, uppercase.
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, border Amber, amber text.
- **Danger**: Solid Rose, white text.
- Fully rounded (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`).

### Alerts & Banners
- Legal warnings (Age verification, operational hours) should use Red/Rose with a shield icon.
- Should appear embedded within the flow, not as intrusive popups.

## 5. Spacing & Layout
- Generous padding and margins to let the content breathe.
- Single column layout on mobile, 2-column or 3-column max on desktop.
- Floating action buttons (FABs) for cart and primary actions on mobile.
